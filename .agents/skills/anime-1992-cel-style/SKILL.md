# 1992 Japanese Anime Cel Mythology Style Guide

This skill documents the signature aesthetic rules, prompt patterns, and rendering techniques for generating authentic 1992 anime production cel art for Indian mythological scenes (Samudra Manthan, Ramayana, Kumbh Mela).

---

## 1. Core Visual DNA (The 1992 Anime Hallmark)

Inspired by the landmark 1992 Indo-Japanese co-production *Ramayana: The Legend of Prince Rama* (directed by Yugo Sako, Ram Mohan, Kazuyuki Kobayashi):

| Aesthetic Dimension | Specification | What to Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Linework** | Crisp, hand-drawn black ink cel outlines; slight line weight variation with organic ink tapering. | Soft airbrushed edges, 3D CGI vectors, glowing digital neon outlines. |
| **Coloring** | Flat gouache cel colors with 2-step or 3-step hard-edged cel shadows. Rich, saturated jewel tones. | Smooth airbrushed gradients, muddy photorealistic textures, plastic digital sheen. |
| **Highlights** | Bright, sharp rim lighting and gouache dot highlights (especially on ocean waves, golden crowns, and jewelry). | Blurry bloom filters, lens flares, ambient occlusion passes. |
| **Water & Elements** | Stylized Japanese anime wave curls with frothing white gouache crests, dynamic sea spray specks, and curling mist. | Generic photorealistic water shaders, transparent 3D glass reflections. |
| **Texture & Grain** | Authentic 35mm film stock cel texture, slight analog warm grain, hand-painted watercolor backdrops. | Over-sharpened digital noise, 8K hyper-realism. |

## 2. Standard Prompt Template Pattern

When generating mythological assets or scene artwork, always build prompts using this strict structural formula:

```text
Master 1992 Japanese anime cel animation production artwork of [SUBJECT / ITEM / SCENE] in the hand-painted style of Ramayana: The Legend of Prince Rama (1992).

[DETAILED SUBJECT BREAKDOWN]:
- Core Entity: [Clear description of deities, creatures, or sacred artifacts with authentic Vedic iconography]
- Attire & Accents: [Saffron orange silks, royal sapphire blue drapes, vermilion accents, carved gold ornaments, pearl malas]
- Motion & Energy: [Dynamic stance, bursting from ocean seafoam, golden celestial god rays, floating sparkles]

[ENVIRONMENT & ATMOSPHERE]:
- Ocean / Stage: Churning milk ocean (Kshira Sagara) with frothing white gouache wave curls, stylized Japanese water crests, and dynamic sea spray.
- Lighting & Sky: Dramatic celestial illumination, hand-painted watercolor clouds, warm radiant halos.

[MEDIUM & CINEMATOGRAPHY]:
- Traditional hand-inked black cel outlines, flat cel-shaded gouache palette, 2-tone cast shadows, 35mm cel film grain, cinematic framing.
```

## 4. Animation Kinematics Pattern (Zero-Tear Rule)

When animating cel layers in web technologies (GSAP, SVG, Canvas):
1. **Unified Fulcrum Anchoring**: Always anchor pivot points at the physical base of support (e.g., Mount Mandara base at Lord Kurma's shell `960px 850px`).
2. **Coupled Motion Displacement**: Never allow independent lateral shear between connected entities (like the serpent Vasuki) to exceed **2 pixels** to prevent visible seam tearing.
3. **Harmonic Sinusoidal Easing**: Use continuous symmetrical waves (`sine.inOut`) for infinite seamless loops without loop-boundary snaps or pauses.
4. **Decoupled Camera Shake**: Always isolate camera shake impulses on an independent outer wrapper (`#cameraShakeRig`) so slow Ken Burns camera drifts remain silky smooth.
